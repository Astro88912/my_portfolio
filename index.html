me

Curious by nature.
Driven to build.

I’m HARIMURALI S S, an S1 Computer Science and Engineering student at ACE College of Engineering, with a curiosity for technology and a passion for turning ideas into something real. I’m particularly interested in web development, cybersecurity, problem-solving, and exploring new technologies. I enjoy understanding how things work, experimenting with unfamiliar concepts, and creating projects that are different from the usual.

I naturally take initiative when I have an idea, and I enjoy leading teams, taking responsibility, and bringing people together to move a project forward. Whether I’m working with C++, Python, Arduino, or learning something completely new, I believe in learning by building and experimenting. Not every idea works the first time—and that’s part of the process. My goal is to keep learning from every attempt, turn unusual ideas into meaningful projects, and grow into a developer who can create, lead, and solve problems that matter.
